How to Join

1. Ask

Contact the club president or a board member that you want to join.

2. Join Google Classroom

Ask the club president or a board member how to join the club's Google Classroom.

3. Get Confirmed

Once you are confirmed, you are on the team.

The joining process may change as the club grows, and could include placement tests. Check with the club president or board member for the current steps.
